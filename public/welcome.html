As we know, necessity is the mother of invention. As a student studying at a rural university, I realized that many students do not have access to online food-ordering facilities on campus. At universities like RGUKT, getting food from campus food courts can be a hectic task, as they are often located far from the hostels. Consequently, many students are reluctant to walk all the way to the food courts to get their meals. To address this problem, I developed a web application that allows students to order food and have it delivered directly to their hostel doorsteps by fellow students. Students who take up delivery orders can earn money for their services, creating a convenient and mutually beneficial system for the campus community.
ABOUT

As a Computer Science student at RGUKT Basar, I developed **OrderByUs** with a simple yet innovative idea: connecting people who need something delivered with those who are willing to deliver it. Students in rural universities and colleges often lack access to online food-ordering facilities. At institutions like RGUKT, campus food courts are sometimes located far from hostels, making it inconvenient for students to collect their meals. Recognizing this everyday challenge, I created OrderByUs as a platform that makes ordering and delivering food easier, faster, and more accessible. The innovation behind OrderByUs lies in its student-to-student delivery model. Instead of depending on external delivery services, the platform connects two groups of users: those who want food or other items delivered and those who are willing to deliver them for a reasonable delivery charge. Customers can place orders, while delivery agents can browse available orders, take them up, and earn money by completing deliveries. OrderByUs is designed to achieve two goals: **saving students' time and creating earning opportunities for fellow students.** It encourages a convenient, collaborative, and financially beneficial campus environment. The platform is completely free to access, with no platform usage charges. Although it was initially inspired by the challenges faced by students in rural universities, its concept can be implemented beyond college campuses wherever people need convenient delivery services. With OrderByUs, my aim is to demonstrate how a simple idea, driven by a real-life problem, can become an innovative digital solution that connects people, creates opportunities, and makes everyday life easier.
